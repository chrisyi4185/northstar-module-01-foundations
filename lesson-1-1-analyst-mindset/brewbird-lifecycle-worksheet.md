**Analyst:** [A. Chris Yi]

**Client:** Brewbird Coffee Roasters
**Stakeholder:** Carmen Ruiz-Santana, CEO

## 1. Define

The question: What is the root cause od declining customer counts at the flagship location that is not affecting other locations

The decision this will inform: Whether to invest in renovation, change management, close the location, or something else

What "done" looks like: Tables and graphs that isolate the real reason customers aren't coming to the flagship location while they are still visiting the other locations

## 2. Collect

Datasets needed: total customer counts for the last 12-18 months from all stores to validate that the counts are indeed down, traffic and construction data for that location for 12-18 months, membership/rewards data for that location for 12-18 months, inventory data for the last 12-18 months, staffing data for the last 12-18 months, data on how many customers enter vs how many actually purchase, sales data across all product types. Customer satisfaction from social media and other sources, order turn around times

Datasets that already exist: sales data and customer data should already exist. customer counts should exist as well. Inventory data should exist. staffing data should also exist

Datasets that don't exist or aren't accessible: Traffic and construction data may not be available. Will need further research to determine if they are available.

## 3. Clean

Cleaning Challenges: There may be duplicate data in the customer tracking if some customers have multiple rewards accounts. Not all datasets will come in using the same formatting so they will need to be cleaned. Some data that is needed to analyze may not be available at all and may need to be collected

## 4. Analyze

Is the customer count the only thing that's dropping?

Are customers coming in and not making a purchase?

Are inventory issues impacting the customers purchase choices?

Are certain items only available at certain locations that would increase sales for that location?

Are staffing issues impacting the turn around times for orders?

Is something specific to the location like weather or traffic/construction impacting the ability of customers to get to the location?

Are customer satisfaction ratings stable or is there change present there as well?

## 5. Visualize

I would like to have a line graph showing the total customer counts for the location firstly. If there isn't actually a drop in the count something else is going on

I need a month over month bar graph showing over time if the change is seasonal and occurred last year as well or is it a new issue we are just discovering now?

A line graph showing overall customer ratings by month would show satisfaction

## 6. Communicate

Upon reviewing the data, we will be able to determine where the decline is coming from. My best first look would assume that something about the specific location itself like a management or inventory issue or access problems outside of the actual business are the most likely culprit. More information is needed to make a clear hypothesis.
